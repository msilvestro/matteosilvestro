1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how One in a Thousand ("the App") handles user information. The App is developed and published by Matteo Silvestro.

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

The App operates entirely offline.

The App does not request access to sensitive device permissions such as:

  • Storage

4. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from users of any age.
